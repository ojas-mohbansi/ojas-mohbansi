01 About

Private AI that runs on your hardware — not someone else's cloud.

I'm a Computer Science undergraduate at IIIT Nagpur (graduating 2028), building AI tools and open-source software since December 2024.

My work spans private AI assistants running on local hardware (Raspberry Pi, home servers), WhatsApp and Discord automation, a civic offline legal aid app for India, and agentic systems that decompose goals and execute plans without calling a cloud API.

TypeScript and Python are my daily languages. I'm drawn to edge inference, self-hosted infrastructure, and the intersection of AI and tools that reach people who can't afford per-message cloud bills.

  1. i.

    Run it locally

    If the model can run on a Raspberry Pi or a homelab server, it doesn't need a cloud subscription. I design for the edge first.

  2. ii.

    Build for people who need it

    Nyaya Sahayak works offline in 17 languages over Bluetooth — because legal aid shouldn't require an internet connection.

  3. iii.

    Make AI answer for itself

    Proto explains every action it takes. An agent that can't justify its decisions isn't useful — it's a liability.
